# My Concierge - Product Management Projects

This repository contains coursework completed as part of the Udacity Product Management Nanodegree.

The projects follow the development of **My Concierge**, a digital hotel room-service ordering concept, from product vision through design validation and product-development planning.

---

## Projects

### 1. Product Vision

Defines the product opportunity, target audience, value proposition, business goals, success measures, and roadmap.

📄 See: `docs/01-product-vision.pdf.pdf`

---

### 2. Design Sprint

Documents the Design Sprint process, including:

- Problem framing
- Sprint focus
- Ideation
- Solution selection
- Prototype development
- User testing
- Updated PRD

📄 See: `docs/02-design-sprint.pdf.pdf`

---

### 3. Managing Product Development

Covers:

- Functional and non-functional testing
- Dogfood survey design
- Fire-drill communications
- Prioritization
- Go / No-Go launch readiness
- Stakeholder communication

📄 See: `docs/03-managing-product-development.pdf.pdf`
